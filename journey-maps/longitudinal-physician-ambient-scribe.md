## Overview
Follows the longitudinal physician persona (composite of participant P09) across all four
ambient-scribe touchpoints she's been part of over the past year.

## Stages
1. **v0.1 (Feb 2025)** — Skeptical, reads every generated line; medication dosage errors present
   in 3 of 5 sessions reinforce that caution.
2. **v0.2 (Sep 2025)** — Errors drop, but reading behavior doesn't — still reads every line
   despite the accuracy gain. A frustration plateau: "why doesn't this feel any different?"
3. **GA Candidate (Jan 2026)** — Per-line confidence highlighting introduces the first behavior
   change: begins skimming high-confidence lines for the first time. Relief, tempered by
   sound-alike medication names still being a known, monitored gap.
4. **Post-GA (Feb 2026)** — The dedicated medication-safety flag reads as more actionable than
   generic confidence highlighting — but only if unflagged lines don't get over-trusted as a
   result. Session lock during dictation remains a trust risk entirely unrelated to transcription
   quality — a "the tool ate my work" scare that isn't actually true.
