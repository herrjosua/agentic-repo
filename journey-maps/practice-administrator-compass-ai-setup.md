# The Practice Administrator — Compass AI Practice Setup Journey

## Overview
**Evidence basis:** Mixed. Observed: what Practice Administrators did and said at each step, from `raw/2026-02-03-usability-test-compass-ai-practice-setup-flow/session-notes.md:43-45,51,53,55,58-65`, and the dashboard counts, from `raw/2026-01-20-dashboard-review-compass-ai-practice-setup-completion/session-notes.md:41-48,51-59`; each stage cites its lines. Inferred: laying the two sources side by side as one journey is this record's framing; the dashboard doesn't show who ran each setup (`:36-38`), so its counts aren't a Practice Administrator's journey, and no session links any of the 24 setups to the test participants.

Follows the Practice Administrator persona
([`personas/practice-administrator-compass-ai-setup.md`](../personas/practice-administrator-compass-ai-setup.md))
through the five steps of the Compass AI practice setup flow, in order, all required in the build
studied (2026-02-03 `session-notes.md:33-37`). Each stage gives what the 4 Practice Administrators
in the usability test (P154-P157) did and said, then what the dashboard recorded for all 24 setups
started 2025-12-01 to 2026-01-16. This map gives no emotion ratings, scores or pain points beyond
what the raw notes state. This is about admin setup of Compass AI, not staff training.

## Stages
1. **Sign in with Meridian SSO.** In the test, "6 of 6 participants completed both without help or
   comment on a problem" (Sign in and Practice details; 2026-02-03 `:55`). Participants used their
   own Meridian SSO (`:39-40`). Dashboard: no setup was open here, because a setup only counts as
   started once someone signs in (2026-01-20 `:57-59`).
2. **Practice details.** Same in the test: completed by 6 of 6 without a recorded problem
   (2026-02-03 `:55`). Dashboard: 1 of 24 setups was open here, for 5 days (2026-01-20 `:44`).
3. **Connect calendar.** In the test, 3 of 4 Practice Administrators (P154, P155, P157) stopped and
   wouldn't connect it, because it gives Compass AI access to patient appointments and they did not
   think they could approve that; each said it was IT's decision. P156 asked the moderator whether
   it was theirs to approve before going on (2026-02-03 `:51`).
   - "That's patient names and visit reasons. That's not my call — that's IT's." — P154 (`:58-59`)
   - "Is this mine to say yes to? I'd normally send anything like this to IT." — P156 (`:61-62`)

   **Completing this step is not a result.** When a participant stopped here, the moderator told
   them it was the sandbox calendar and let them continue; "completing step 3 in this session is not
   a result" (`:43-45`), and all 4 Practice Administrators continued only that way (`:51`). The map
   doesn't show what a Practice Administrator would do next on their own practice's real calendar.
   Dashboard: 7 of 24 setups were open here, for 4 to 41 days (median 16), the most of any step; in
   the 12 finished setups it was the step left open longest in 8 of 12 (2026-01-20 `:45`, `:51-54`).
   The designer's stated rationale for requiring this step, that the scribe attaches recordings to
   scheduled encounters (2026-02-03 `:38`), is untested.
4. **Invite your team.** In the test, 3 of 4 Practice Administrators (P154, P156, P157) hesitated:
   unsure whether the email goes out when they finish the step or at Review & finish, and not
   wanting staff emailed before champion training is booked for the practice. P155 entered the test
   staff list and went on without comment (2026-02-03 `:53`). The step used a prepared test staff
   list, so no real staff were emailed (`:41-42`).
   - "If forty people get an email before anyone's booked training, my phone rings all
     afternoon." — P157 (`:64-65`)

   Dashboard: 3 of 24 setups were open here, for 6, 11 and 19 days (2026-01-20 `:46`).
5. **Review & finish.** **The study recorded no result for this step beyond the dashboard
   counts.** The usability test notes record nothing that participants did or said here, though the
   facilitation guide had probes for it
   (`facilitation-guides/compass-ai-practice-setup-usability-guide.md:65-67`). Dashboard: 13 of 24
   setups reached Review & finish, 12 finished and 1 was open here for 2 days (2026-01-20 `:42`,
   `:47-48`). Whether the invite email is sent at this step or at step 4 isn't recorded (2026-02-03
   `:46-47`).

## Gaps
- The dashboard doesn't show the signed-in person's role (2026-01-20 `:36-38`), so its counts may
  include setups run by IT Administrators.
- Why the dashboard setups stopped isn't recorded (2026-01-20 `:72-74`); the test participants'
  reasons can't be matched to them.
- What happens after setup with a real schedule and real staff isn't covered: nothing real was read
  or sent (2026-02-03 `:41-42`).
