## Summary
Dana, 34, Office Manager at a 40-person company. Just been handed Fernway
setup as "one more thing" on top of her regular job. Not technical, but
capable — she just doesn't have time to figure things out by trial and error.

## Goals
- Get the team up and running with minimal back-and-forth.
- Avoid looking like she broke something in front of her team.

## Frustrations
- Steps that don't clearly say what's required vs. optional.
- No way to preview what happens before committing to an action.
