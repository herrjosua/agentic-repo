## Objectives
Understand where new workspace admins get stuck during first-time setup, and
whether the current 6-step onboarding wizard matches how admins actually
think about the task.

## Method
Moderated remote usability sessions, 45 minutes each, think-aloud protocol.
Sample: 6 participants, all first-time Fernway admins hired within the
last 90 days, mix of company sizes (10–200 employees).

## Timeline
- Recruiting: Jan 12–16
- Sessions: Jan 19–23
- Synthesis: Jan 26–30
