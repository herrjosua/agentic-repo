## Quick takeaways
- Clinicians shown confidence highlighting skim high-confidence lines at roughly double the rate
  of the pre-highlighting cohort — directionally consistent with the GA-candidate usability
  finding that visible uncertainty, not raw accuracy, changes reviewing behavior.
- Adoption is uneven by role: attending physicians are opting in at a noticeably higher rate than
  residents in the same departments.

## Open questions
- Is the physician/resident adoption gap a training gap, a trust gap, or a workflow difference?
  Dashboard data alone can't answer this — flagged as a candidate for a short follow-up interview
  round.
