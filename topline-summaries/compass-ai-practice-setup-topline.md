# Compass AI Practice Setup — Study Topline

## Quick takeaways
**Evidence basis:** Observed. Every takeaway is stated in `raw/2026-01-20-dashboard-review-compass-ai-practice-setup-completion/session-notes.md:36-48,72-74`, `raw/2026-02-03-usability-test-compass-ai-practice-setup-flow/session-notes.md:32,38,43-48,51-55,58-59,64-65,74-79` or `raw/2026-02-24-interview-it-administrator-schedule-access-and-staff-invites/session-notes.md:26,29,40-41,44`, and each line cites its own. Two caveats repeat what the finding and the analytics summary already say about their limits, and cite them.

One page on the three sessions about how an administrator sets Compass AI up for a practice: a
dashboard review (2026-01-20), a moderated usability test with 4 Practice Administrators (P154-P157)
and 2 IT Administrators (P158, P159) (2026-02-03, `session-notes.md:32`), and an interview with
one IT Administrator (P160) (2026-02-24). Synthesized in
[`compass-ai-practice-setup.md`](../research/findings/compass-ai-practice-setup.md). This is about
admin setup of Compass AI, not staff training.

- **Setups stop most at Connect calendar, then at Invite your team.** Of 24 setups started
  2025-12-01 to 2026-01-16, 12 had finished and 12 were still open on 2026-01-20: 7 at Connect
  calendar (open 4 to 41 days, median 16), 3 at Invite your team (6, 11 and 19 days), 1 at
  Practice details and 1 at Review & finish; 13 of 24 reached Review & finish (2026-01-20
  `:41-48`). The number of practices in the network isn't recorded, so there is no completion rate
  (`:38-40`). See
  [`compass-ai-practice-setup-funnel.md`](../analytics/summaries/compass-ai-practice-setup-funnel.md).
- **Practice Administrators don't think the calendar is theirs to approve.** 3 of 4 (P154, P155,
  P157) stopped at Connect calendar and each said it was IT's decision; P156 asked the moderator
  whether it was theirs to approve before going on (2026-02-03 `:51`).
  > "That's patient names and visit reasons. That's not my call — that's IT's." — P154 (`:58-59`)
- **IT Administrators connect it, but want to know what is read.** Both P158 and P159 connected
  the calendar only after asking which fields are read; the screen doesn't list them and the
  moderator couldn't answer (`:52`).
- **Practice Administrators don't want staff emailed before training is booked.** 3 of 4 (P154,
  P156, P157) hesitated at Invite your team, unsure whether the email goes out at that step or at
  Review & finish; P155 went on without comment (`:53`).
  > "If forty people get an email before anyone's booked training, my phone rings all afternoon."
  > — P157 (`:64-65`)
- **Only IT can request the privacy review schedule access needs.** In P160's account, any new
  system that reads the appointment schedule needs a privacy review request first, and a Practice
  Administrator can't start one; they didn't know whether one had been filed for Compass AI
  (2026-02-24 `:29`).
- No issues at Sign in or Practice details: 6 of 6 completed both (2026-02-03 `:55`).

## Recommendations as recorded
From 2026-02-03 (`:74-79`): list the fields read on Connect calendar; add a "Send to IT for
approval" path there; say on Invite your team when the invite email is sent; let administrators
schedule the invite email for a date. From 2026-02-24 (`:40-41`): route an administrator who can't
approve schedule access to IT; before more practices set up, confirm with IT whether a privacy
review request has been filed and list the fields it covers.

## Caveats
- Completing Connect calendar in the test is not a result: the moderator let anyone who stopped
  continue on a sandbox calendar (2026-02-03 `:43-45`).
- The calendar step is required because, in the designer's statement at session setup, the scribe
  attaches recordings to scheduled encounters (`:38`). That is the designer's stated rationale; no
  session tested it (finding `:42-44`).
- The dashboard doesn't show who ran each setup or why it stopped (2026-01-20 `:36-38`, `:72-74`),
  so the reasons above can't be matched to the 24 setups, and the analytics summary isn't
  independent evidence for them (summary `:39-41`).
- The interview is one IT Administrator's account of the approval process, not a review of its
  records (2026-02-24 `:26`).

## Open questions
- When does this build send the invite email, and how is Compass AI access provisioned? Neither is
  recorded (2026-02-03 `:46-48`).
- Has a privacy review request for Compass AI's schedule access been filed, and which fields does it
  cover (2026-02-24 `:44`)? Which scheduling system holds the schedule isn't recorded either
  (`:26`).
