## Overview
Not a persona — a mindset that shows up across multiple admin personas.
Defined by low time budget and a "just get it done" orientation, regardless
of technical skill level.

## Implications
Design for skimmability over completeness: fewer required decisions, clearer
signaling of what's mandatory, and no dead-ends that force starting over.
