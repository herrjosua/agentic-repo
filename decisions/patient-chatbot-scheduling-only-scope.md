# Patient chatbot scoped to scheduling, without symptom questions

## Decision
Scope the patient chatbot's initial functionality strictly to scheduling and logistics, and
exclude symptom-related questions from v1
(`research/raw/2025-06-03-survey-patient-portal-ai-chatbot-trust/session-notes.md:41`).

Decision status: proposed. The survey recommended this scope (same file, `:41`), and on
2025-06-17 a "scheduling-only chatbot prototype" was usability tested
(`research/raw/2025-06-17-usability-test-patient-chatbot-prototype/session-notes.md:21`). Those
lines show a recommendation and a prototype built to it; no record says the scope was adopted for
the product or shipped. The finding calls this "the decision"
(`research/findings/patient-scheduling-chatbot.md:28-30`), but it names no decider or date, and the
raw records show only a recommendation and a prototype built to it.

## Alternatives considered
- Include symptom-related questions in v1: recommended against, "given the sharp trust drop-off"
  (`research/raw/2025-06-03-survey-patient-portal-ai-chatbot-trust/session-notes.md:41`).

## Rationale
**Evidence basis:** Observed. Each claim below is stated in the cited raw session lines.

The rationale on record is the survey's, not a decider's. 67% of respondents were comfortable with
an AI chatbot for scheduling and rescheduling, but comfort dropped to 22% when the question
extended to answering questions about their symptoms
(`research/raw/2025-06-03-survey-patient-portal-ai-chatbot-trust/session-notes.md:29`). The finding
says this "directly shaped the decision to scope the chatbot to logistics only"
(`research/findings/patient-scheduling-chatbot.md:28-30`).
