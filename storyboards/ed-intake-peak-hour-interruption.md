## Scenario
A registration clerk and an intake nurse work a peak-hour ED shift where interruptions land every
2-4 minutes — call lights, incoming ambulance radio traffic, a physician question mid-registration.

## Panels
1. Nurse starts intake on a new patient; gets interrupted by a bedside alarm two questions in.
2. Returns, has lost track of where she was in the form — a tool requiring sustained review
   attention would compound this, not help it.
3. Registration clerk, a lower-acuity task, snaps a photo of an insurance card — pre-fill happens
   instantly, no sustained attention required.
4. Contrast panel: the clerk's task fits an AI assist well; the nurse's doesn't — not because of
   the AI's quality, but because of the interruption rate itself.
5. Recommendation callout: carve out insurance-card pre-fill as a smaller, separate, lower-risk
   feature; leave ED intake nursing out of near-term rollout.
