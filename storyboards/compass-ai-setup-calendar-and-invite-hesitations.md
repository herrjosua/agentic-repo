## Scenario
**Evidence basis:** Mixed. Observed: what happens and is said in each panel is stated in `raw/2026-02-03-usability-test-compass-ai-practice-setup-flow/session-notes.md:39-40,43-47,51-53,55,58-59,61-62,64-65,82-84` and `raw/2026-02-24-interview-it-administrator-schedule-access-and-staff-invites/session-notes.md:26,29,36-37,44-45`, and each panel cites its lines. Illustrative: combining the 4 Practice Administrators (P154-P157) into one character, and telling it as one sequence, is this record's framing; no session follows one person this way.

One unnamed Practice Administrator, drawn from the 4 in the 2026-02-03 usability test (P154-P157),
sets Compass AI up for their practice. Each quote stays with the participant who said it. Not every
participant did everything shown: 3 of 4 stopped at Connect calendar and 3 of 4 hesitated at Invite
your team, not the same three (2026-02-03 `session-notes.md:51`, `:53`). The storyboard gives no
setting, time of day or interruptions, because none are recorded, and no outcome. This is about
admin setup of Compass AI, not staff training.

## Panels
1. **Signing in.** The administrator signs in with their own Meridian SSO and completes Practice
   details. No trouble: "6 of 6 participants completed both without help or comment on a problem"
   (`:39-40`, `:55`).
2. **Connect calendar: they stop.** Connecting gives Compass AI access to patient appointments,
   and they don't think it's theirs to approve (`:51`).
   > "That's patient names and visit reasons. That's not my call — that's IT's." — Practice
   > Administrator, P154 (`:58-59`)
3. **Whose call it is.** They ask whether it's theirs to say yes to, and say who they would
   normally send it to (`:51`).
   > "Is this mine to say yes to? I'd normally send anything like this to IT." — Practice
   > Administrator, P156 (`:61-62`)

   IT's side, as one IT Administrator described it on 2026-02-24 (not a review of its records,
   `2026-02-24 session-notes.md:26`): a privacy review request comes first, and only IT can file
   one (`:29`).
   > "A practice admin can't put in that request. They call us, we file it." — IT Administrator,
   > P160 (`:36-37`)
4. **Not shown: getting past Connect calendar.** In the test, "All 4 continued only on the
   sandbox calendar" (2026-02-03 `:51`): when a participant stopped at step 3, the moderator told
   them it was the sandbox calendar and let them continue, a test accommodation, so "completing
   step 3 in this session is not a result" (`:43-45`). The storyboard
   doesn't show the administrator connecting the calendar, or what they would do on their
   practice's real one.
5. **Invite your team: they hesitate.** They aren't sure whether the email goes out when they
   finish the step or at Review & finish, and they don't want staff emailed before champion
   training is booked for the practice (`:53`).
   > "If forty people get an email before anyone's booked training, my phone rings all
   > afternoon." — Practice Administrator, P157 (`:64-65`)
6. **Open questions.** The storyboard ends here, on what nobody in the study could answer:
   - When is the invite email sent? The step 4 screen doesn't say, and the notes don't record when
     this build sends it (`:46-47`, `:84`).
   - Which fields does Connect calendar read? The screen doesn't list them (`:52`).
   - Has a privacy review request for Compass AI's schedule access been filed, and which fields
     does it cover? (2026-02-24 `:44`)
   - How is Compass AI access provisioned: through SSO groups, through these invites, or both?
     (2026-02-03 `:82-83`; 2026-02-24 `:45`)
