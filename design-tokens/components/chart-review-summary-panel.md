## Notes
Concept follows the 2025-01-29 baseline contextual inquiry's recommendation to scope an early AI concept around 'what changed since last review' rather than full summarization, given coordinators' existing distrust of the EHR's auto-generated after-visit summaries. One coordinator (P05) asked for "what changed since I last touched this chart".

Since refined by the 2026 coordinator interviews, with the panel still a concept. The reference point for "what changed" should be the care team's last contact with the patient, whoever made it, rather than the coordinator's last touch of the chart or the patient's last visit: P05, now floating, has often never touched the chart on a covering shift (2026-04-07), and P130 anchored it on their own last outreach call (2026-03-03). It should cover appointments and results as well as medications, since for referral follow-up a new appointment or a returned result is usually the change that matters (P137, 2026-09-29).
