# Compass AI Practice Setup — Usability Test Facilitation Guide

## Session setup
**Evidence basis:** Inferred. A script built from the study plan (`research-plans/compass-ai-practice-setup-study-q1.md`), which focuses on the two steps where the 2026-01-20 dashboard review found setups stopping most (`raw/2026-01-20-dashboard-review-compass-ai-practice-setup-completion/session-notes.md:51-56`, `:65-67`). It holds no results.

Moderated, remote, think-aloud; 60 minutes; one participant per session. Moderator: Priya Patel.

Before the participant joins:
- Confirm the test environment is up: the sandbox calendar for step 3 and the test staff list for
  step 4 are in place, so no real schedule is read and no real staff are emailed.
- The designer states the rationale for the calendar step: it is required because the scribe
  attaches recordings to scheduled encounters. This is context for the moderator. Don't share it
  with the participant or ask them about it; the study doesn't test it.
- Have the notes template open, with a space for questions the moderator couldn't answer.

## Introduction (about 5 min)
- Thanks for joining. We're looking at the flow an administrator uses to set Compass AI up for a
  practice. We're testing the flow, not you.
- Please think aloud as you go: what you're looking at, what you expect, what you're unsure of.
- You'll sign in with your own Meridian SSO. Please set up as you would for your own practice.
- It's a test environment, so nothing you do here reaches a real schedule or real staff. I'll
  mostly stay quiet while you work.

## Warm-up (about 5 min)
- Tell me about your role and what you look after for your practice.
- When a new system needs setting up for your practice, what does that usually involve, and who
  else is part of it?

## Tasks (about 40 min)
Give each task in turn. Don't name the next step before the participant reaches it. Spend most of
the time on steps 3 and 4.

**Step 1 — Sign in with Meridian SSO.** "Start setting up Compass AI for your practice."
- Probe: What did you expect to see after signing in?

**Step 2 — Practice details.** "Carry on as you would."
- Probe: Is there anything here you'd need to check with someone first?

**Step 3 — Connect calendar.** "Carry on as you would."
- Before they act: What do you think this step does? What would connecting it give Compass AI?
- If they pause: What are you thinking about right now? What would you need to know to go on?
- After they finish or stop: How did that step feel compared with the ones before?

**Step 4 — Invite your team.** "Carry on as you would."
- Before they act: What do you think happens when you complete this step?
- Who would you put on this list, and what do you expect them to receive?
- After they finish or stop: How did that step feel compared with the ones before?
- If they pause: What are you thinking about right now? What would you need to know to go on?

**Step 5 — Review & finish.** "Finish the setup."
- Probe: Is anything here different from what you expected?
- Probe: What do you expect happens next, for you and for your staff?

## Moderator rules
- **Accommodation at step 3:** if the participant stops at Connect calendar and won't go on, tell
  them it is the sandbox calendar and let them continue. Note that the accommodation was used and
  what they said before it. Completing step 3 is then not a result.
- **Questions you can't answer:** the moderator doesn't know when this build sends the invite
  email (at step 4 or at Review & finish), or how Compass AI access is provisioned (through SSO
  groups or through these invites). If a participant asks either, or anything else about the build
  you don't know, say you can't answer, note the question word for word, and let them decide how to
  go on.
- Don't explain a step's purpose or say what other participants did.
- Don't steer toward or away from completing a step; stopping is something to observe, not fix.
- Neutral prompts only: "What are you thinking?", "What would you expect?", "Say more about that."

## Wrap-up (about 10 min)
- Looking back, which step took the most thought, and why?
- Is there anything you'd need to have in place at your practice before doing this for real?
- If you could change one thing about this flow, what would it be?
- Anything we didn't cover that you'd like to add?
- Thank the participant and close the session.
