## Warm-up
- Tell me about your role and how long you've been using Fernway.
- Walk me through the last time you set up a new workspace for your team.

## Core questions
- (Task) Imagine you just signed up — walk me through what you'd do first.
- What does "workspace" mean to you before you've used the product?
- At each step: what do you expect to happen next, before you click?

## Wrap-up
- What was the most confusing part of getting set up?
- If you could change one thing about this flow, what would it be?
- Thanks — anything you'd like to add that we didn't cover?
