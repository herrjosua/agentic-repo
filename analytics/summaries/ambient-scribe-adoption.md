# Ambient scribe adoption

## Summary

After-hours charting time for ambient-scribe cohort clinicians dropped
only modestly (~9% median) despite high self-reported perceived time
savings in the linked usability study — consistent with clinicians
re-reading generated notes rather than trusting them outright.

## Source

`analytics/raw/2026-06-02-ambient-scribe-usage-export/snapshot.csv`,
pulled from internal-usage-telemetry, 5-clinician pilot cohort.
