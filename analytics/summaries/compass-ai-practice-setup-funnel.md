## Summary
In the Compass AI practice setup flow, where every step is required, setups stop most at step 3
("Connect calendar") and stay there longest, then at step 4 ("Invite your team"). This summary
covers where setups stopped and for how long; it doesn't say why.

## Data
Sourced from a manual review of the internal usage dashboard for the practice setup flow, recorded
in `research/raw/2026-01-20-dashboard-review-compass-ai-practice-setup-completion/session-notes.md`.
The window is setups started 2025-12-01 to 2026-01-16, with step status as of 2026-01-20 (`:29-31`).
The dashboard export itself isn't in the repo; the figures below are the ones that review recorded.

- 24 setups started (a setup starts when someone signs in at step 1; `:37-38`).
- 12 of 24 finished; 12 of 24 were still open on 2026-01-20 (`:44-45`).
- Still open, by step: Practice details 1 of 24 (5 days); Connect calendar 7 of 24 (4 to 41 days,
  median 16); Invite your team 3 of 24 (6, 11 and 19 days); Review & finish 1 of 24 (2 days)
  (`:46-49`).
- 13 of 24 reached Review & finish (`:50`).
- In the 12 finished setups, Connect calendar was the step left open longest in 8 of 12 (`:55-56`).

The number of practices in the network isn't on the dashboard or in the corpus, so there's no
completion rate against all practices (`:40-42`). The dashboard shows setups by practice, not the
role of the person who ran each one (`:39-40`).

## Interpretation
Connect calendar is the main place setups stall, by count and by time, and Invite your team is
second. The dashboard can't say why (`:74-76`). For reasons, see
[compass-ai-practice-setup.md](../../research/findings/compass-ai-practice-setup.md), which draws on
a usability test and an interview. This summary is not independent of that qualitative evidence: the
same review prompted the usability test, and nothing here links any of the 24 setups to the reasons
given by the 6 test participants or the 1 interviewee.
