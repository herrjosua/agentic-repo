# Onboarding funnel drop-off

## Summary

Step 2 (permissions) is the largest single drop-off point in the funnel
at 27.7%, roughly 4-5x every other step. Steps 1, 3, and 4 are all under
6% drop-off.

## Source

`analytics/raw/2026-09-11-onboarding-funnel-export/snapshot.csv`, pulled
from dummy-analytics-platform, 10,000-user cohort.

## Relation to qualitative research

This confirms the pattern seen in the 2026-09-11 moderated usability test
(`research/raw/2026-09-11-onboarding-flow-usability-test/`) — step 2 was
the stall point for 4 of 5 participants there too. See
`research/findings/onboarding.md` for the combined synthesis.
