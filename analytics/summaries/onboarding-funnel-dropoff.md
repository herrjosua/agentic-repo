## Summary
Step 3 ("Connect calendar") is the single largest drop-off point in the
onboarding funnel, accounting for a larger share of incomplete signups than
any other step.

## Data
Sourced from the Jan 2026 onboarding funnel export (platform analytics,
not included in this fictional dataset — a real repo would keep the
untouched export under `analytics/raw/2026-01-onboarding-funnel-export/`).

## Interpretation
Drop-off concentrated at step 3 independently corroborates the usability
study's finding (`research/findings/onboarding.md`) that step 3's wording
is misread as optional — this isn't users abandoning due to task difficulty,
it's users not realizing the step is required and exiting instead of
completing it.
