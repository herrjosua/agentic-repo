## Concept
**Evidence basis:** Mixed. Observed (the test results for options 1 and 2, from `raw/2026-02-10-ambient-scribe-medication-flag-concept/session-notes.md:43-45`); the options themselves, and dropping option 3 before testing, are the designer's decisions and aren't in that session's notes.

Quick sketches exploring where the sound-alike-medication flag should sit relative to the
medication name, ahead of the 2026-02-10 concept test.

## Options considered
1. Inline pill immediately after the medication name — carried forward; won 5:1 in testing since
   participants didn't want to look away from the text.
2. Right-hand margin badge, mirroring the layout of other dashboard flags — tested as an
   alternate and lost; inline placement was preferred 5:1 because participants didn't want to
   look away from the text to check a flag.
3. Modal interrupt on encountering a flagged line — the designer's decision to drop it before
   testing, as too disruptive for a signal meant to prompt verification, not force a stop. It
   wasn't tested, so there's no participant result for it.
