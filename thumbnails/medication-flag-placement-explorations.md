## Concept
Quick sketches exploring where the sound-alike-medication flag should sit relative to the
medication name, ahead of the 2026-02-10 concept test.

## Options considered
1. Inline pill immediately after the medication name — carried forward; won 5:1 in testing since
   participants didn't want to look away from the text.
2. Right-hand margin badge, mirroring the layout of other dashboard flags — tested, lost; required
   a separate glance that broke reading flow.
3. Modal interrupt on encountering a flagged line — dropped before testing; too disruptive for a
   signal meant to prompt verification, not force a stop.
