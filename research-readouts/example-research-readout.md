## Summary
New admins drop off most at the calendar-connection step, not because the
step is hard, but because it's unclear it's required. Funnel data confirms
the same step as the largest single drop-off point.

## Key findings
- Step 3 wording implies optionality it doesn't have — see finding
  `onboarding`.
- Progress indicator is present but unused; participants navigate by scroll,
  not by the indicator.

## Recommendations
- Relabel step 3 to state clearly that calendar connection is required.
- Consider removing the sidebar progress indicator or replacing it with
  inline step numbers, since it isn't being used as designed.
