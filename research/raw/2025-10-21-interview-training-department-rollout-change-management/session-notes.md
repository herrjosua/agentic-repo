# Interview — Training & Education Dept on Rollout/Change Management Planning

## Objective
Understand what a realistic training/rollout plan looks like for the first Compass AI features going to general availability, drawing on lessons from past rollouts.

## Method
- **Method:** 1:1 interview, 40 min
- **Researcher:** M. Okafor
- **Full participant roster:** see `participants.md` in this folder

## Key Findings
- **[MEDIUM]** *(past rollout lesson)* Director pointed to the 2022 CDS alert rollout (also referenced by executives in January) as a cautionary tale specifically because training was a single 20-minute video with no hands-on practice, and adoption suffered as a result — she wants any Compass AI rollout to include live, hands-on sessions instead.
- **[MEDIUM]** *(champion model)* Prefers a 'unit champion' model (1-2 respected staff per unit trained early and deeply, who then support peers) over an all-at-once broadcast rollout, based on past success with an unrelated EHR module rollout in 2023.
- **[LOW]** *(timing sensitivity)* Flagged that rolling out during flu season (Nov-Feb) has historically correlated with poor training attendance and should be avoided if possible for the ambient scribe GA rollout.

## Representative Quotes
> "We already know what doesn't work. A video nobody watches and hoping for the best. I don't want to repeat 2022."
> — Director of Clinical Training & Education, P99

> "Give me two respected nurses per unit, trained early and given actual time to practice, and they'll do more for adoption than any all-staff email ever will."
> — Director of Clinical Training & Education, P99

## Recommendations
1. Build the ambient scribe GA rollout plan around the unit-champion model, and explicitly avoid flu-season timing given the historical attendance pattern.
2. Include hands-on practice time in training, not just a recorded overview, directly addressing the 2022 lesson learned.

## Follow-ups / Open Questions
- Get the actual 2022 CDS rollout adoption numbers if they exist, to quantify the lesson rather than rely on recollection.
- Identify candidate unit champions once the GA rollout unit list is finalized.
