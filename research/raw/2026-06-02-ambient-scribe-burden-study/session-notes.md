# Ambient scribe documentation burden study

## Objective
Understand whether the ambient scribe feature is reducing after-hours
documentation time for clinicians, and where trust in the AI-generated
note breaks down.

## Method
- **Method:** Moderated session, 6 clinicians, think-aloud review of a
  real (de-identified) ambient-scribe-generated note against their own
  edits
- **Researcher:** J. Bock

## Key Findings
- **[HIGH]** *(trust)* 4 of 6 clinicians re-read the entire generated
  note line-by-line rather than skimming, citing concern about missed or
  fabricated details — this erodes the time savings the feature is meant
  to provide.
- **[MEDIUM]** *(workflow)* Clinicians want a faster accept/reject
  affordance for individual note sections rather than all-or-nothing
  approval.
