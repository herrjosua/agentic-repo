# Survey — All-Staff AI Readiness Pulse Survey

## Objective
Take an org-wide temperature check on AI readiness/sentiment heading into 2026, as input to roadmap prioritization and communications planning.

## Method
- **Method:** Org-wide anonymous pulse survey, 10 questions
- **Researcher:** M. Okafor
- **Full participant roster:** see `participants.md` in this folder

## Key Findings
- **[MEDIUM]** *(awareness gap)* Only 34% of non-clinical staff reported being aware Compass AI initiatives were underway at all, versus 71% of clinical staff — suggesting internal communications have been clinically focused and may need broadening if non-clinical rollouts (e.g., scheduling) proceed.
- **[MEDIUM]** *(sentiment trend (directional, not causal))* Among clinical respondents, favorable sentiment toward AI-assisted documentation was somewhat higher than the March-ish baseline period, though this survey used different question wording than February's, so it's directional context rather than a clean comparison.
- **[LOW]** *(top requested feature)* When asked what AI feature they'd most want next, 'appointment scheduling help' was the top write-in among non-clinical/admin staff, and 'documentation time savings' remained the top pick among clinical staff — consistent with priorities identified all year.

## Representative Quotes
> "I hear about this AI stuff from clinical friends but nobody's told admin staff what's actually happening or if it affects us."
> — Non-clinical/administrative staff, P112 (survey open-ended)

## Recommendations
1. Broaden internal communications about Compass AI to non-clinical staff specifically before any admin-facing pilot (e.g., scheduling chatbot) goes live, given the awareness gap found here.
2. If a clean sentiment trend is wanted for 2026 reporting, re-run the exact February nursing survey instrument rather than a new pulse survey, to get a true apples-to-apples comparison.

## Follow-ups / Open Questions
- Decide with leadership whether the September burnout survey or a repeat of the February survey is the better instrument for the planned 6-month post-GA comparison.
