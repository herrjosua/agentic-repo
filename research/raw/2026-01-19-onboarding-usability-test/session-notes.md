**Researcher:** Priya Patel

## Session 1 — Jan 19
Participant paused ~15s at step 3 ("Connect calendar"), said "wait, do I
have to do this?" before proceeding. Completed step without connecting a
real calendar (used the skip-adjacent "I'll do this later" affordance,
which doesn't actually exist — participant tried clicking the step label
itself as an exit).

## Session 2 — Jan 20
Attempted to skip step 2 directly, assuming it was optional. Backed out
when the "required" indicator appeared, expressed mild frustration
("could've told me that up front").

## Session 3 — Jan 21
Same step 3 hesitation as session 1. When asked directly, said: "I guess
I'm worried this is going to email my whole team before I'm ready."

## Sessions 4–6 (Jan 22–23)
Consistent with sessions 1–3: step 3 wording read as optional by all
participants; none independently identified it as required without
hitting the "required" state first.
