# Survey — Clinician Burnout & Documentation Burden Baseline

## Objective
Establish a pre-AI-rollout baseline of self-reported burnout and documentation burden, so future surveys can measure whether Compass AI tools move the needle.

## Method
- **Method:** Anonymous org-wide survey, 15 questions (validated burnout subscale + custom documentation items)
- **Researcher:** Priya Patel
- **Full participant roster:** see `participants.md` in this folder

## Key Findings
- **[HIGH]** *(documentation as burnout driver)* Documentation burden ranked as the #1 self-selected contributor to burnout among physicians specifically (52%), ahead of patient volume (31%) and administrative meetings (12%).
- **[MEDIUM]** *(after-hours charting)* 64% of physician respondents report regularly completing documentation after clinic hours ('pajama time'), averaging a self-reported 1.2 hours/day.
- **[MEDIUM]** *(baseline caution)* Respondents were told this survey relates to a future AI initiative, which may have primed more favorable-than-baseline attitudes toward documentation-reducing tools — worth noting as a methodological caveat for anyone comparing to the February nursing survey, which didn't mention AI in its framing.

## Representative Quotes
> "I love this job. I do not love finishing my notes at 9pm after my kids are in bed. If AI fixes that, I'm in."
> — Physician, P84 (survey open-ended)

> "Just don't let 'AI will fix documentation' become the excuse to skip fixing the actual EHR problems underneath it."
> — Nurse Practitioner, P91 (survey open-ended)

## Recommendations
1. Use this survey as the official baseline metric for measuring Compass AI's impact on documentation burden and after-hours charting in future re-surveys (e.g., 6 and 12 months post-rollout).
2. Note the framing caveat explicitly in any report comparing this dataset to the February nursing survey.

## Follow-ups / Open Questions
- Schedule a repeat of this exact survey instrument 6 months after ambient scribe GA rollout for comparison.
