# Executive Steering Committee — Year-End Retro & 2026 Roadmap Input

## Objective
Close out the year with the same executive group (plus two additions) from the January kickoff to review what research found, what shipped, and what should be prioritized in 2026.

## Method
- **Method:** Group interview/retro, 90 min, 4 executives
- **Researcher:** J. Alvarez
- **Full participant roster:** see `participants.md` in this folder

## Key Findings
- **[MEDIUM]** *(shared metrics gap partially closed)* The group agreed, informally, to adopt 'documentation time saved' and 'PHI incidents (target: zero)' as the two headline metrics going into 2026 — resolving the shared-metrics gap flagged as a finding back in January, though it's not yet been formally written into any governance document.
- **[MEDIUM]** *(recurring platform debt surfaced)* The CIO specifically called out the color-only status indicator issue (found independently in 3 separate 2025 audits) as an example of the kind of small, recurring finding that research should flag as a pattern, not just three separate tickets — asked for a standing 'cross-cutting findings' section in future reporting.
- **[LOW]** *(scope discipline praised)* VP Clinical Informatics specifically praised the decision to keep behavioral health and complex prior-auth cases out of scope for 2025, saying the discipline of 'not doing it' was as valuable as anything shipped.

## Representative Quotes
> "A year ago we couldn't agree on what success even meant. Now we at least agree on the two numbers we're arguing about. That's real progress."
> — Chief Information Officer, P01 (returning from January)

> "The stuff you told us not to build yet mattered as much as the stuff you told us to build."
> — VP Clinical Informatics, P02 (returning from January)

## Recommendations
1. Formalize the two agreed 2026 headline metrics in an actual governance document before Q1 2026 planning locks, so the informal agreement here doesn't get lost.
2. Introduce a standing 'cross-cutting findings' section in the research repo/reporting specifically to surface patterns like the color-only status issue across otherwise-separate sessions.

## Follow-ups / Open Questions
- Draft the formal metrics document and route for exec sign-off in January 2026.
- Retrofit a 'cross-cutting findings' index into the research repo covering 2025 (e.g., color-only status, transparency/citation requests, Part 2 flagging gap).
