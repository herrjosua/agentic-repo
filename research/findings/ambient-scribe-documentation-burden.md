# Ambient scribe documentation burden

## Current understanding

Trust, not accuracy, is the bottleneck. Clinicians largely trust the
ambient scribe's transcription but not its judgment about what to
include, so most re-read the full note anyway — undercutting the
intended time savings. Usage analytics corroborate this: measured
after-hours charting time barely moved for high-usage clinicians, even
though self-reported "time saved" is high (see linked analytics
summary).
